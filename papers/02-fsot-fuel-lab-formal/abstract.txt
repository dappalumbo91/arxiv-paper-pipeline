Engineering fuel design typically mixes empirical curve fits with many free parameters. We present a focused application of Fluid Spacetime Omni-Theory (FSOT): the same seed-derived scalar engine (raw_S = term1 + term2 + term3 from pi, e, phi, gamma, and Catalan's G) used in the companion formal-cosmology paper, evaluated on a Fuel Lab live panel of 366 thermochemistry and engine-simulator records with zero per-observable least-squares tuning.

Empirically, the Fuel_Lab_Live_Panel achieves a pooled median error of 0.039349% under a 0.5% green gate, versus an 8% typical operational baseline for fuel-property error in the repository's SOTA comparison. Seven FSOT-designed alternative fuels (hemp grounded/advanced, algae biodiesel, mushroom ester, green hydrogen, optimax, bio_spark) plus a gasoline baseline are cross-checked against seed-scalar predictions. Formally, Fuel Lab priors export into the multi-prover obligation spine (Lean primary); verified-desktop cross-proof closure reports VERIFIED_DESKTOP_CROSS_PROOF_READY with oracle replay deltas of 0.0 on sampled properties. Preregistered PRED-034 locks the thermochemistry panel discriminant before refresh.

This paper does not re-argue the full 402-domain atlas or contested H0 sector.
